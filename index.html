Working together

You don’t need a finished
brief to start.

Start with what you want people to learn and the resources you already have. We can help you decide what to build on and what to develop.

  1. Agree what people need to learn

    We discuss who the program is for and how it will be taught. We agree what to include and what your team will contribute.

  2. Review a sample lesson

    We develop a lesson or module for your team to review. We adjust the content and format before developing the rest.

  3. Develop the full program

    We write and design the remaining materials. We agree review points so your team can check the work as it develops.
